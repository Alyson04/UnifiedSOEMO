PUP Red Cross Youth Council (PUP RCYC)

PUP Red Cross Youth Council (PUP RCYC) is an extension of one of the Philippine Red Cross’s services: Red Cross Youth and Volunteering services. Under the Red Cross Manila Chapter Youth Council, PUP RCYC serves as a humanitarian organization that promotes the values and ideas of PRC and develops RCY programs and other Red Cross services within the Polytechnic University of the Philippines.

How Students Can Join Our Organization:

  • The student must be enrolled full-time at the Polytechnic University of the Philippines and are interested in being a volunteer for the Red Cross can join the organization.
  • The student must fill out the organization’s membership form to become a full-fledged member of PUP RCYC.

    • Follow the PUP Red Cross Youth Council (PUP RCYC) Facebook Page.
