PUP Philippine Studies Student Association

PUP PhilSSA is an academic organization for BA Philippine Studies students, focused on engaging with social, economic, and political issues while building a supportive community.

Objective:

  • To lead in promoting Philippine Studies and Area Studies as a progressive multidisciplinary and interdisciplinary Social Science program within the University's academic community;
  • And to publish research papers, execute programs, projects and participate in discussions that expands the knowledge and interest of students within the university and the general public.

How Students Can Join Our Organization:

    This organization is currently available only for the students in the university who are enrolled in BA PHILIPPINE STUDIES. If you belong in the program and been wanting to join, you can contact the given officers/execomm of the org. Or you may reach out to PUP PhilSSA's official FB Page.

    Follow the PUP PhilSSA Facebook Page.
