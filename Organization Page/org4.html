AWS Cloud Club PUP

Vision: As a pioneering Student Organization centered around Amazon Web Services and Cloud Computing, we envision a long-standing community of passionate learners from various universities across the country who embrace innovation and leverage the power of Amazon Web Services to drive technological advancements.

Mission: We are committed to actively participating in community service initiatives and developing a well-rounded perspective on the importance of contributing to society. Valuing the spirit of volunteerism and striving to create opportunities for students to give back and create meaningful change. We strive to empower students to specialize in their desired field and its practical applications by delivering inclusive educational initiatives, interactive workshops, and avenues for networking. We serve as the medium to equip students with the necessary tools they need to thrive in the digital economy and contribute to the transformation of industries through AWS.

How Students Can Join Our Organization:

    The AWS Cloud Club PUP opens its membership application at the start of every semester. Any bona-fide student of PUP from all PUP Campuses nationwide is welcome to join. Here are the steps in becoming an AWS Cloud Club PUP:

  • Follow the AWS Cloud Club PUP Facebook Page.
  • When open, answer the membership application form to be posted on the AWS Cloud Club PUP Facebook Page. The usual requirements are as follows and may vary each academic year:
    • Full Name and Basic Information
    • Contact Information
    • Social Media Links
    • Academic Information
    • Certificate of Registration (or any Proof of Enrollment)
  • Expect to receive an email within a week after the membership application form closes.
  • Once you have received your acceptance email and your club ID, you are now officially an AWStronaut with AWS Cloud Club PUP.
