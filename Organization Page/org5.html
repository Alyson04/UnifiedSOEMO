PUP Seeds of the Nations

The Seeds of the Nations (SONs) is a campus-based ministry, which exists to develop young people in their character, knowledge, skills and vision.

Purpose: The ultimate purpose of the existence of PUPSONs as an organization is to love God and to love people (Matthew 22:36-40). The SONs exist to reach unchurched students, to connect them with other Christians, to help them grow in their faith, and to challenge the growing, to discover their ministry and to honor God with their lives.

Vision: To win souls and make disciples Goal: To make every believer a leader of leaders.

How Students Can Join Our Organization:

    Attendance does not mean membership. Membership means being accountable and responsible for fellow Christians with a commitment to grow and adherence to the SONs purpose statement. Just send us a message if you want to be part of our community and we will reach out to you.

    Follow the PUP Seeds of the Nations Facebook Page.
