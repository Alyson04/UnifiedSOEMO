#PUPCET2025

January 12, 2025

Friendly reminder to all PUPCET takers

  • Print your test permit in COLOR in advance.
  • Be at the testing center 30 minutes before your schedule.

What to bring:

  • ✓ Printed test permit
  • ✓ Current High School ID
  • ✓ No. 2 Mongol Pencil (bring an extra)
  • ✓ Confidence, dahil kaya mo 'to!

What NOT to bring:

  • ✗ Bag
  • ✗ Calculators
  • ✗ Food and drinks
  • ✗ Electronic/communication devices
  • ✗ Envelope

NOTE: Latecomers will not be allowed to take the test or enter once it starts.
